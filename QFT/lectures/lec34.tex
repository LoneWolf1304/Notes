\section*{Lecture 33: Spinor Yukawa Theory}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Lecture 33: Spinor Yukawa Theory}
We have seen interactions only between scalar fields uptil now. We had also seen fermion fields and gauge fields earlier. Hence we will discuss what happens when fermions get coupled to scalar fields, which is the actual \textit{Yukawa theory}. The interaction is of the form 
$$\SH\tund{int} = g \phi\dadj{\Psi}\Psi$$
where $\Psi$ is a Dirac spinor and $\dadj{\Psi}$ is the Dirac adjoint. We needed to make a Lorentz invariant scalar and hence the simplest interaction, as seen before, had to be of this form. The entire Lagrangian becomes 
$$\SL = \frac{1}{2}(\partial^\mu \phi)(\partial_\mu \phi)-\frac{1}{2}m^2\phi^2+\dadj{\Psi}(i\gsl{\partial}-M)\Psi -g \phi\dadj{\Psi}\Psi$$
