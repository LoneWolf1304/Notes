\section*{Lecture 29: Introducing Interactions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Lecture 29: Introducing Interactions}
So far we had been discussing the free field theory, where there was no interactions between the particles. We had a hint of interactions emerging when we tried to impose local gauge symmetry on the Lagrangian, when two-point and four-point interaction terms automatically emerged by the use of covariant derivatives.
