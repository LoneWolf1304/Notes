\begin{question}[Yang-Lee edge]

 Consider the following grand partition function for a hypothetical fluid
\[
\Xi(z,V) = (1+z)^{[\alpha V]}(1+z^{[\alpha V]}).
\]
Here $[x]$ denotes the integer part of $x$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Determine the zeros of $\Xi(z,V)$ and (roughly) plot them on the complex $z$ plane. Here $z$ denotes the fugacity, $z=e^{\beta\mu}$.

\item[(b)] Show that the zeros of $\Xi(z,V)$ in the thermodynamic limit hit the real $z$ axis at $z=1$.

\item[(c)] Evaluate
\[
\Theta = \lim_{V\to\infty} \frac{1}{V}\ln\Xi(z,V)
\]
for $z<1$ and $z>1$ and show that it is continuous but non-analytic.

\item[(d)] Given that the particle density is $n = z\frac{\partial \Theta}{\partial z}$, show that there is a density jump at the phase transition.

\item[(e)] Using the fact $\beta p = \Theta$, derive the equations of state $p(n)$, for $z<1$ and $z>1$ by eliminating $z$ from the expressions of $p(z)$ and $n(z)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{question}
(a) To find the roots of $\Xi$, we set 
\begin{equation}
    (z+1)^{[\alpha V]} = 0 \qquad z^{[\alpha V]} = -1
\end{equation} 
The real root is $z=-1$ with multiplicity $[\alpha V]$,  which is physically irrelevant since the fugacity is $z=e^{\beta \mu} > 0$. The other complex roots come from $z^{[\alpha V] } = -1$, which are the roots of $-1$, lying on the unit circle 
$$z_m = e^{i\theta_m}\qquad \theta_m = \frac{(2m+1)\pi}{[\alpha V]}, \ m \in \{0,1\ldots [\alpha V]-1\}$$ \\[0.2cm]
(b) We see that the number of roots increases as we increase $V$. Consider the root with $m=0$ and $n=[\alpha V]-1$:
$$z_0 = e^{i\pi/[\alpha V]} \qquad z_1 = e^{i\pi(2[\alpha V]-1)/[\alpha V] }$$
(c) From the definition, 
\begin{align}
  \begin{split}
      \Theta &= \lim\limits_{V\to \infty} \frac{1}{V} \ln \Xi(z,V) \qquad z \in \re_{\geq 0} \\
&= \lim\limits_{V\to \infty} \frac{1}{V} \qty{[\alpha V]\ln (1+z) + \ln\qty(1 + z^{[\alpha V]})}
  \end{split}
\end{align}
