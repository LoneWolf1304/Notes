\noindent
Now, we can define $\tan \theta = \frac{\psi_2}{m\tund{0}+\psi_1}$. Considering the fluctuations to be small, we can effectively take $\tan \theta \approx \theta$ and $\abs{\phi_1}\ll \abs{m\tund{0}}$ which gives us $\phi_2 \simeq m\tund{0}\theta$. Then the transverse fluctuation is completely characterised by $\theta$.
