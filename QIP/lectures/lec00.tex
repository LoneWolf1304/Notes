\section*{Lecture 00: Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Lecture 00: Introduction}
The course pertains to the topics in quantum information and how to process this information. Quantum computing has recently garnered a huge hype in both academia and industries and the potential applications are being studied. We will deal with the basic introduction to some aspects of quantum information theory in this course.\\[0.2cm]
Although plethora of literature is available for the subject, the references for the course will be:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Quantum Computation and Quantum Information}; \textit{Michael Nielsen, Isaac Chuang}. Termed as the `bible' of quantum information.
    \item \textbf{Preskill Notes}; \textit{John Preskill}. The link to the notes page is \url{www.preskill.caltech.edu/ph229/}
    \item \textbf{The Physics of Quantum Information: Quantum Cryptography, Quantum Teleportation, Quantum Computation}; \textit{Bouwmeester, Ekert, Zeilinger}
\end{itemize}
The grading for the course consists of $20\%$ from the midsem, $20\%$ from two best out of three class tests, $30\%$ from the endsem, $10\%$ from class participation and rest $20\%$ is contributed either from assignments or class notes or both.
